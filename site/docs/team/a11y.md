# Accessibility, motion, performance and compliance checklist

Denise Frank site. Developer implements, auditor verifies. Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Every bullet is a ship gate.

## 1. Contrast (WCAG formula, computed by scratchpad/a11y/contrast.mjs)

```
token   hex      bg       surface  surface2  rule
                 #072018  #0d2a20  #133a2c
ink     #f4ebdd  14.46    12.98    10.65     body text anywhere
ink2    #c8c1b0   9.53     8.55     7.02     body anywhere; captions, legal
ink3    #9c9a8a   6.02     5.41     4.43     body on bg/surface; on surface2 large text (24px, or 18.66px bold) or decorative only
brand   #d4a843   7.72     6.93     5.68     text and UI anywhere
profit  #e8c874  10.53     9.45     7.75     text anywhere
safe    #4cc38a   7.71     6.92     5.68     text anywhere, always with a word or icon
loss    #e36a55   5.26     4.72     3.87     body on bg/surface; large text or UI only on surface2; never the only signal
info    #7fb2d0   7.47     6.71     5.50     text anywhere
rare    #e08a4a   6.43     5.77     4.73     text anywhere
```
Thresholds: body 4.5:1, large text 3:1, UI parts and focus rings 3:1.

- [ ] Gold button: bg #072018 on #d4a843 = 7.72:1 (AAA). Every filled button (gold, profit 10.53, safe 7.71, loss 5.26) uses bg ink. Cream on gold is 1.87:1, on coral 2.75:1: never.
- [ ] line #1f4a39 (1.71:1) and surface2 (1.36:1) on bg are decorative only. Edges a user must perceive (inputs, chips, slider track, card edge, toggles) use ink3 or brand.
- [ ] Footer cream opacity floor for 4.5:1: 50% on bg (#7e867b), 52% on surface, 57% on surface2. Code floor 60% (5.93:1); ink2 floor 63%. Prefer solid ink2.
- [ ] Grain, halation and vignette stay at 0.08 opacity or less over text; verify by screenshot, axe cannot see canvas.

## 2. Keyboard

- [ ] First two tab stops, visible on focus: "Skip to main content", "Skip to the closing table" (the permanent HUD exit, a real `<a href="#closing-table">`).
- [ ] DOM order = chapter order = tab order. GSAP pins never reparent. Unrevealed content is `inert` until revealed; under reduced motion everything is revealed at load.
- [ ] A pinned or scrubbed scene jumps to `progress(1)` on `focusin` from any descendant, so nobody tabs into an invisible control.
- [ ] Chapter arrival (HUD links, "Next room", hash): Lenis scrolls, then focus moves to the section `<h2 tabindex="-1">`. `scroll-padding-top` and the Lenis offset equal the HUD height, so focus is never hidden behind it.
- [ ] Roving tabindex (one tab stop per group; arrows move; Home/End jump; Enter/Space activate; Tab exits): town chips (`role="radiogroup"` "Pick your town", `role="radio"`, `aria-checked`) and Library cards (`role="group"` "Word of the Day cards").
- [ ] Lab sliders: native `<input type="range">` with visible `<label>` and `<output>`. Steps: rent $50, price $5,000, rate 0.125, down payment 1%. Arrow 1 step, PageUp/PageDown 10, Home/End limits. `aria-valuetext`: "$2,000 per month", "$350,000 home price", "6.5 percent rate", "20 percent down".
- [ ] Escape collapses an open card (focus returns to its button), closes the HUD menu, dismisses a toast or the finale. Never scrolls or navigates.
- [ ] Canvas: `aria-hidden="true"`, no `tabindex`, no OrbitControls, no key listeners, never `canvas.focus()`. Hero headline and CTA are DOM above it.
- [ ] HUD is `<nav aria-label="Keyring">`; toggles are buttons with `aria-pressed`. Keyboard scrolling stays native; Lenis handles wheel and touch only.
- [ ] No `outline: none` without the section 7 ring. Focus is trapped only inside the open finale, returned on close.

## 3. Screen readers

- [ ] Landmarks: `<header>` (logo, skip links), `<nav aria-label="Keyring">`, `<main>` with eight `<section aria-labelledby>` rooms, `<footer>` (disclosures). One `<h1>` in the Porch: "Denise Frank. Not your usual open house." One `<h2>` per room, one `<h3>` per interaction ("Rent or buy?", "Spot the euphemism", "Word of the day", "Pick your town", "Meet the supervisors", "Talk to Denise").
- [ ] One live region, present from first paint, never removed: `<div role="status" aria-live="polite" aria-atomic="true" id="announcer">`. Clear, then write after 50 ms. Toasts are visual mirrors, not live regions. No assertive region.
- [ ] Strings: "Key earned: {Room}. {n} of 8 keys." / "Correct. {Phrase} usually means {meaning}." / "Not quite. Try another word." / "All 8 keys earned. The Closing Table is open." / "Sound on." / "Sound off." / "Motion reduced." / "Motion on." / "Progress restored: {n} of 8 keys." Slider values live in `<output>`, not the announcer.
- [ ] Keyring: `<p>` "3 of 8 keys" plus an `<ol>` of eight links named "{Room}, key earned" or "{Room}, no key yet", current room `aria-current="true"`. Not a progressbar: keys are discrete and navigable; a progressbar would double-announce.
- [ ] Names: "Sound", "Reduce motion" (`aria-pressed`), "Skip to the closing table", "Next room: {Room}", "Dismiss", card = the word ("Escrow", `aria-expanded`), "Rico, in real life" / "Rico, in real estate life", "Denise on Facebook (opens in new tab)". Icons `aria-hidden="true"`; names from text or `aria-label`, never `title`.
- [ ] Decoration: canvas, particles, grain and vignette get `aria-hidden="true"` and `pointer-events: none`; decorative SVG `aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"`. A visually hidden sentence describes the hero: "A front porch at dusk in Conroe, Texas."
- [ ] Flip card: `<button aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="def-escrow">Escrow</button>` then `<div id="def-escrow" hidden>`. The flip is CSS keyed on `[aria-expanded="true"]`; back text is real DOM. No CSS-only hover flip.
- [ ] Euphemism game: each round a `<fieldset>` with `<legend>` "Which word is hiding something?"; candidates `<button aria-pressed>`; the result is visible text that also goes to the announcer. Wrong answers keep every button enabled.
- [ ] Photo alt carries the joke: "Cheeto, an orange and white cat, in a black suit beside a black SUV."

## 4. Motion and vestibular safety

- [ ] One source of truth: `motionReduced = storedToggle ?? matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches`, mirrored as `html[data-motion="reduced"]`. Toggle persists in localStorage `hr-motion`, overrides the OS both ways, is announced.
- [ ] Reduced mode: parallax 0; no ScrollTrigger pin or scrub (sections stack, native scroll); Lenis not initialized; no scroll snap; particles static; text reveals are opacity crossfades of 200 ms or less; flips instant; key-earned a 150 ms fade; Three.js renders one frame then `setAnimationLoop(null)` (re-render on resize only). Implement in `gsap.matchMedia()` keyed on both the media query and the attribute.
- [ ] Nothing flashes: no luminance change more than three times per second, no full-screen flashes. Hero idle drift under 20 px/s; the Reduce motion button is on screen from first paint (the 2.2.2 pause control).
- [ ] Finale: one shot, 2.5 s max, drifting gold particles, no strobe, no screen-wide fill, dismissible by Escape or a button, never covering Closing Table controls. Reduced mode: static "All 8 keys" card plus the announcement.
- [ ] Sound off until the visitor turns it on; nothing autoplays with audio.

## 5. Cognitive and game accessibility

- [ ] No timers, countdowns, scores or session end. Wrong answers cost nothing; tries are unlimited.
- [ ] Keys reward participation, never correctness, and never lock content: contact details, disclosures and every room are readable without playing.
- [ ] "Skip to the closing table" is visible at every scroll position on every viewport, 44 px tall, in the fixed HUD.
- [ ] Progress persists in localStorage `hr-keys`, restores on load with the "Progress restored" announcement, clears only through "Start over" with a confirm step.
- [ ] Identical key feedback in all eight rooms: same gold key icon, toast placement, announcer pattern, optional sound cue.
- [ ] One instruction per interaction, 15 words max, directly above the control, linked with `aria-describedby`: "Drag the sliders. See what owning really costs." / "Tap the word that hides the truth." / "Flip a card to learn the word." / "Pick your town." / "Tap a photo to see the other life."
- [ ] Copy at Flesch-Kincaid grade 8 or lower, Reading Ease 60 or higher, sentences of 20 words or fewer, jargon defined on first use. Legal footer exempt. Readability check runs in `npm test` over the copy JSON.
- [ ] HUD order and labels identical in every room. No context change on focus or input: sliders never navigate, chips never scroll.

## 6. Pointer and touch

- [ ] Targets at least 44 by 44 CSS px with 8 px between neighbors: chips, cards, HUD buttons, toast dismiss, footer links (padding, not line height), slider thumbs (28 px visual, 44 px hit). 2.5.8 asks 24; we commit to 44.
- [ ] No hover-only content: meanings, captions and reveals open on tap, click and focus; hover only mirrors focus styles.
- [ ] Drag alternatives: sliders are native ranges (tap the track, or keyboard); photo pairs toggle on tap, no compare handle; the hero has no drag that carries information.
- [ ] Actions fire on `click` or `pointerup`, never `pointerdown`.
- [ ] Lenis: `smoothWheel: true`, `syncTouch: false` (native touch), keyboard untouched. No CSS `scroll-snap`. Hash links push one history entry per deliberate navigation, scrolling never pushes history, `history.scrollRestoration = 'auto'`, Back returns to the previous room at its position.
- [ ] Viewport meta has no `user-scalable=no` or `maximum-scale`; both orientations work.

## 7. Forms and links

- [ ] Phones as visible digits inside tel links: `<a href="tel:+18329283019">(832) 928-3019</a>` (Denise, direct), `<a href="tel:+18326610475">(832) 661-0475</a>` (office). No invented email: link the contact form and the HAR appointment page.
- [ ] Link text states purpose alone ("Book time with Denise on HAR"), never "click here". Body links gold and underlined.
- [ ] Focus ring: `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--ink); outline-offset: 2px; }`. Cream against bg 14.46:1, surface 12.98:1, surface2 10.65:1, line 8.48:1. Against a gold fill 1.87:1, so filled buttons add `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--bg)` as the gap, keeping the ring adjacent to bg only. Outline, not box-shadow, so forced-colors mode keeps it.
- [ ] External links open in the same tab by default. TREC documents and social profiles open in a new tab with `rel="noopener"`, visually hidden "(opens in new tab)", and "(PDF)" in visible text where it applies. External icon `aria-hidden`.
- [ ] Any native form: visible labels, `autocomplete` on name, tel and email, text errors tied with `aria-describedby` and announced, no placeholder-as-label, no redundant entry, no CAPTCHA puzzle.

## 8. Performance budget (an accessibility matter)

- [ ] JS gzipped, CI size check after `vite build` fails over 350 KB total. Measured here: a minimal tree-shaken scene (renderer, camera, plane, shader material, points) through three 0.186 and Vite 8 is 139 KB; the whole library is 417 KB. So: named imports from `three` only, no `three/examples/jsm` (no OrbitControls, EffectComposer, GLTFLoader), no `three/webgpu` or TSL; grain and vignette live in CSS or the one scene shader. gsap plus ScrollTrigger 46 KB, lenis 6 KB, app 60 KB max. Three loads in its own chunk via dynamic `import()`.
- [ ] LCP 2.5 s or better on a mid phone (Moto G class, throttled 4G, Lighthouse mobile). LCP element is the hero headline in DOM, painted before any WebGL. Critical CSS inline under 14 KB, no render-blocking script, hero poster `<img fetchpriority="high">` AVIF under 60 KB.
- [ ] Fonts: self-hosted woff2 latin subsets, `font-display: swap`, display face preloaded, size-adjusted fallbacks generated by the `fontaine` Vite plugin from `@capsizecss/metrics`: `@font-face { font-family: "DM Sans fallback"; src: local("Arial"); size-adjust; ascent-override; descent-override; line-gap-override }` for DM Sans, DM Serif Display, JetBrains Mono. CLS under 0.1.
- [ ] WebGL initializes in `requestIdleCallback` after `load`, canvas sized by CSS beforehand (no shift), poster fallback when WebGL is unavailable.
- [ ] Save-Data or `prefers-reduced-data`: poster instead of WebGL, system fonts, smaller `srcset`. `deviceMemory` or `hardwareConcurrency` of 4 or less: particles at one quarter, `setPixelRatio(min(dpr, 1.5))`. Scene pauses when the tab is hidden or the canvas is off screen.
- [ ] Images carry width and height, `loading="lazy"` below the fold, `decoding="async"`, 120 KB max. INP under 200 ms: scrub updates in rAF only, no layout reads in scroll handlers.

## 9. Texas real estate and Fair Housing compliance

Footer block, exact text, visible without interaction at 14 px or larger (TREC asks 10 pt), reachable from every room through the exit link:

> **Hometown Realtors of Texas LLC**
> Denise Frank, Broker/Owner. Texas Real Estate Commission broker license #0530671.
> 1104 Jacob's Lake Blvd., Conroe, TX 77384. Direct (832) 928-3019. Office (832) 661-0475.
> [Texas Real Estate Commission Information About Brokerage Services](https://www.har.com/mhf/terms/dispBrokerInfo?sitetype=aws&cid=530671)
> [Texas Real Estate Commission Consumer Protection Notice (PDF)](https://content.harstatic.com/pdf/TREC_CPN.pdf)
> Equal Housing Opportunity. Hometown Realtors of Texas LLC follows the federal Fair Housing Act and the Texas Fair Housing Act. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status or national origin.
> REALTOR® is a registered trademark of the National Association of REALTORS®. Denise Frank is a member of the Houston Association of REALTORS®.

- [ ] TREC link labels read exactly as above (the rules name them). Brokerage name appears in the footer and the Porch, no smaller than half the largest contact text on the page (TREC 535.155, 531.18, 531.20). Equal Housing logo beside the statement, `alt="Equal Housing Opportunity"`.
- [ ] Lab disclaimer, visible beside the outputs, tied with `aria-describedby`: "These numbers are estimates for learning. They are not a loan quote or financial advice. Your taxes, insurance, HOA, PMI and rate will differ. Talk to a licensed lender before you decide."
- [ ] Credit copy: "Credit to Keys is general information, not credit repair or financial advice. Results differ. Denise is not a credit counselor." Never "boost", "fix" or "guarantee" a score or approval.
- [ ] No guarantees about rates, appreciation or outcomes; no invented statistics, reviews or sources; no em dashes.

Fair Housing copy rules (Map Room notes and all chapter copy; banned list goes into `scripts/lint-copy.mjs`):
1. Describe places, prices, taxes, commutes and the process. Never who lives somewhere or who it is "for".
2. Banned: family-friendly; perfect for families, couples, singles, professionals, retirees, empty nesters; bachelor pad; "ideal for" any group.
3. Banned: safe, low crime, quiet neighborhood, desirable area. Crime is never characterized.
4. Schools are facts only: district and campus names; never "good", "great" or "top" schools; never "for your kids".
5. Banned: exclusive; private community (unless the legal name); upscale neighbors; "people like you".
6. No religious or national origin references: no churches as landmarks, no "ethnic" or "diverse" area labels.
7. Disability: describe features (one story, step-free entry, 36 inch doors), never "not suitable for" or "handicap".
8. Familial status: no "no kids", "adults only", "mature" or "quiet" unless a documented 55+ community; "nursery" becomes "bedroom".
9. Rental and Credit to Keys copy states written criteria that apply to everyone; no "we prefer" or "ideal tenant".
10. Photos show property, places, Denise, Rico and Cheeto, never groups of people chosen to suggest who belongs.
